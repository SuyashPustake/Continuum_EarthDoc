# 1. PROJECT DETAILS


## 1.1 Summary Description of the Project

**Project Name**: GreenCharge California Network

This project involves the installation and operation of a comprehensive network 
of 150 Level 2 (7.2-19.2 kW) and 50 DC Fast Charging (50-150 kW) stations across California 
to support electric vehicle adoption. The charging infrastructure will be strategically 
deployed at shopping centers, office buildings, and public parking facilities. The project 
will displace fossil fuel vehicle miles traveled, resulting in significant greenhouse gas 
emission reductions estimated at 3,143 tCO2e annually.

This project applies VM0038 v1.0 "Methodology for Electric Vehicle Charging Systems" to quantify greenhouse gas emission reductions.

**Project Category**: Transport

# 4. QUANTIFICATION OF GHG EMISSION REDUCTIONS


## 4.1 Baseline Emissions

**Formula**: BE = VMT × EF_FF / FE

### Calculation:

- Vehicle Miles Traveled (VMT): 15,000,000 miles/year
- Baseline Fuel Economy (FE): 25.0 mpg
- Fossil Fuel Emission Factor (EF_FF): 8.78 kg CO2e/gallon

**Baseline Emissions Calculation**:
BE = VMT × EF_FF / FE / 1000
BE = 15,000,000 × 8.78 / 25.0 / 1000
BE = 5,268.00 tCO2e/year

The baseline scenario represents the situation without the project activity.

## 4.2 Project Emissions

**Formula**: PE = EC × EF_grid

### Calculation:

- Electricity Consumed (EC): 8,500,000 kWh/year
- Grid Emission Factor (EF_grid): 0.00025 tCO2e/kWh

**Project Emissions Calculation**:
PE = EC × EF_grid
PE = 8,500,000 × 0.00025
PE = 2,125.00 tCO2e/year


## 4.3 Leakage

Leakage sources have been assessed per methodology requirements. Potential leakage includes:
- Displacement of activities to other locations
- Market effects
- Upstream/downstream emissions

Total leakage is estimated to be <1% of baseline emissions and is considered negligible per methodology guidance.


## 4.4 Net GHG Emission Reductions

**Annual Net GHG Emission Reductions**:
ER = BE - PE - Leakage
ER = 5,268.00 - 2,125.00 - 0 (negligible leakage)
ER = 3,143.00 tCO2e/year

**Total Crediting Period Reductions**:
Total = 3,143.00 tCO2e/year × 10 years
Total = 31,430.00 tCO2e
